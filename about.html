About

QR Generator runs completely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored on a server, and there is no sign-up.

Private by design

Your links, logos and images never leave your device. The QR code is drawn locally on a canvas.

Built to scan

Print-ready

Export PNG (with transparency) or JPG up to 4096 px — sharp enough for posters and packaging.

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes. No watermark, no account, no limits. The QR codes you create are static, so they never expire.

Will my designed QR code scan?

Keep strong contrast between code and background, avoid oversized logos, and always test-scan before printing. The studio shows a live scan-safety badge.

Can I add my logo?

Yes — pick a built-in icon (WhatsApp, Facebook, website and more) or upload from your gallery. Keep it under about 25% for best results.

Which formats can I download?

PNG and JPG at 512, 1024, 2048, 3072 or 4096 px.

What can a QR code contain?

Website links, plain text, WhatsApp chat, Wi-Fi login, UPI payment, email and phone numbers.
